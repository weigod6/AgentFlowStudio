# AFS Phase 1 Evidence Matrix

Access date: 2026-07-15. This is a compact research gate, not a vendor claim
catalog or a legal conclusion.

Evidence labels:

- `P`: official product documentation, API documentation, help center, or
  official repository.
- `G`: official platform or parent-company policy that is relevant but not
  product-specific.
- `V`: official marketing, pricing, trust-center, or vendor-reported statement.
- `U`: no directly verifiable public evidence found.

All cited evidence below was read from public official pages. No logged-in
product workflow was used, so a public page never proves behavior hidden behind
an account, plan, region, or enterprise contract. Vendor-reported performance
numbers are excluded from AFS product claims.

## Research gate conclusion

The market evidence does not support “AFS wins because competitors are generic”
or “AFS wins because it has more agents or a canvas.” The Phase 2 opportunity is
more specific: one creator-visible path from a changed story or continuity fact
to affected shots, an explicit update scope, exact-version review, recovery,
rollback, and traceable delivery. The three prototypes must test that path, not
three visual skins.
